Text layout & legibility

.text-balance for short titles · .text-pretty for body copy — progressive enhancement (text-wrap).

Do not apply balance globally — expensive. Limit to headings / short blocks (≈6–10 lines). pretty is limited in Firefox (ignored → default wrap).
