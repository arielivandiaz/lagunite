Light-dismiss a dialog

closedby="any" closes the modal on backdrop click (and Esc). Always open with showModal(). Safari needs a small click fallback today.

Open the dialog, then click the dimmed backdrop or press Esc.

Native dialog + Lagunite .modal regions.

Feedback

Click outside this box or press Esc to dismiss.

Hero classes / attrs

  • dialog.modal + closedby="any"
  • showModal() (not non-modal show())
  • form method="dialog" for close buttons
  • Legacy: overlay click on .modal-overlay is app JS — prefer native when possible
