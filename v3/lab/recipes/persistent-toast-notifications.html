Bucket 1/2 · MWG persistent-toast-notifications

Persistent toasts

Use popover="manual" so toasts do not light-dismiss. Stack with --toast-offset (top-layer); close with popovertargetaction="hide" or a timer calling hidePopover(). Non-popover stacks can use .toast-stack.

Prefer .toast-stack over absolute sibling offsets — top-layer popovers ignore parent z-index.

Hero classes

  • .toast · .toast-stack (non-popover stack) · .toast-body · .toast-message
  • [popover].toast with popover="manual" + --toast-offset
  • Close: .alert-close + popovertargetaction="hide"
